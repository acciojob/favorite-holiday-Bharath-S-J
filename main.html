Paris, France


Paris, the capital of France, is known for its rich history, stunning architecture, romantic ambiance, and world-class cuisine. It’s a top destination for travelers worldwide.

  1. Eiffel Tower – The most iconic landmark in Paris.
  2. Louvre Museum – Home to the famous Mona Lisa and many other masterpieces.
  3. Notre-Dame Cathedral – A gothic masterpiece with centuries of history.
  4. Seine River Cruise – A beautiful way to explore the city.
  5. Champs-Élysées – A luxurious avenue perfect for shopping and strolling.
  • Croissant – A buttery and flaky pastry you must try fresh from a Parisian bakery.
  • Crêpes – Thin pancakes served with sweet or savory fillings.
  • Macarons – Delicate and colorful French confections.
  • Ratatouille – A delicious vegetable stew native to France.
  • Escargot – A French delicacy made of cooked snails, often with garlic butter.
